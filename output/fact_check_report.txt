CANADA — THE SETTLED BRO ILLUSION
FACT-CHECK REPORT (prepared 17 Nov 2026)
====================================================================

Method
------
* Every number that appears on screen was checked against a primary source where one
  was reachable (canada.ca / IRCC, Budget 2026, Statistics Canada releases), otherwise
  against at least one named secondary source that cites the primary data.
* The original Telugu narration was NOT changed. Where the narration is outdated,
  unverified or simplified, the on-screen graphic either (a) shows the verified figure
  with its date, or (b) labels the figure "as stated in narration" / "estimate".
* Status codes:  VERIFIED · VERIFIED-SECONDARY · CORRECTED-ON-SCREEN · UNVERIFIED (labelled)
  · NARRATION-ONLY (not shown as a statistic)

1. The 5 November 2026 announcement / 200,000 measure ............... VERIFIED
   Source: IRCC backgrounder "Building a stronger Canada through a well-managed,
   predictable immigration system", canada.ca, 2026-11-05 (captured on screen).
   - 380,000 permanent resident admissions per year, 2027–2029.               VERIFIED
   - 370,000 new temporary resident arrivals per year (220,000 workers; up to
     150,000 international students).                                          VERIFIED
   - "Time-limited measure" to help up to 200,000 people already living in Canada
     transition to PR, processed through 2027: workers cohort up to 100,000 (provincial/
     territorial nominations incl. PNP, and applicants already in certain federal
     programs); humanitarian cohort up to 100,000 (remaining Hong Kong & Ukraine family
     pathway applications, approved H&C cases, spouses/partners/children of Canadians and
     PRs already in Canada).                                                   VERIFIED
   - It is NOT automatic PR; eligibility rules were not fully published at time of
     writing. The video says so explicitly ("NOT AUTOMATIC PR", "full rules not yet
     published").

     "time-limited". On-screen wording uses "time-limited".
